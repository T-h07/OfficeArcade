# Product Concept

## What OfficeArcade Is

OfficeArcade is a workplace-friendly desktop gaming platform for short, structured multiplayer breaks inside a company environment.

## Target Audience

- Companies that want lightweight, policy-controlled social play during breaks
- Employees who want quick, low-friction game sessions with coworkers
- Internal administrators who need governance over access and usage

## Employee Version vs Admin Version

- Employee version focuses on play participation: joining rooms, hosting sessions, progression, and social reputation.
- Admin version focuses on control and compliance: user access, departments, room policies, and cooldown governance.

## Respect, Karma, and Cooldown Concepts

- Respect and Karma are reputation signals intended to reward healthy participation and social behavior.
- Cooldown policies limit overuse and encourage balanced break-time play.
- Exact scoring, eligibility rules, and enforcement logic are intentionally deferred to later OA-PT phases.
